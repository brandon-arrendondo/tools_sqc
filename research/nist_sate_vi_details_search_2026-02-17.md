### Key Findings

- SATE VI is the sixth occurrence, workshop September 2019.
- SATE is a non-competitive study (tools are typically anonymized).
- SATE VI injected realistic vulnerabilities into large test cases.
- Includes Ockham Criteria Track focused on sound static analysis tools.
- Tools differed significantly in precision and recall.
- Tools' precision and recall ranking varied for different weaknesses.
- Specific numerical detection rates for individual tools NOT available in search results.
- Full report needed for quantitative data.
