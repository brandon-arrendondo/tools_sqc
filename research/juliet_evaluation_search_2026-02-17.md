### Key Findings

- Juliet Test Suite 1.1: 81,000+ synthetic C/C++ and Java programs with known flaws, covering 181 CWEs.
- SATE IV used Juliet 1.0 (~60,000 test cases). NIST analyzed ~180,000 warnings for Juliet by automated means.
- Astree found thousands of unintended defects in Juliet 1.3, highlighted for "outstanding precision" (no false positives).
- SATE VI used Ockham Sound Analysis Criteria to evaluate tools.
